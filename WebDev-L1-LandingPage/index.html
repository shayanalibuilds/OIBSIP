On the desk

  • Warm

    2700K behind linen. The page is lit. The rest of the room stays dim.

  • Simple

    A brass wheel with four stops. No app. No pairing. A switch on the cord as well.

  • Steady

    A short arm and a heavy oak base. It does not wander when the desk moves.

02

From a spare room in Karachi

We were tired of lamps that looked finished in a photograph and felt thin at midnight. Oak & Ember is a small run of lamps assembled by hand: turned oak, brushed brass, a shade you can lift off without tools.

Fifty pieces in the first batch. Then we stop and review the next cut of wood.

It lights the page and leaves the room alone.Mira S., Lahore
